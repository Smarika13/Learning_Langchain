Nepal is a landlocked country in South Asia, bordered by China to the north and India to the south, east, and west. It is home to eight of the world's ten tallest mountains, including Mount Everest, which stands at 8,848 meters above sea level. The country covers an area of approximately 147,181 square kilometers and has a population of around 30 million people.

Kathmandu is the capital and largest city of Nepal. It serves as the political, cultural, and economic hub of the country. The Kathmandu Valley contains seven UNESCO World Heritage Sites, including Pashupatinath Temple, Boudhanath Stupa, and Swayambhunath. These ancient monuments attract thousands of tourists every year.

The economy of Nepal relies heavily on agriculture, tourism, and remittances. More than 60 percent of the population is engaged in agriculture. Tourism contributes significantly to the GDP, with trekking and mountaineering being the most popular activities. The Annapurna Circuit and Everest Base Camp trek are among the most famous trekking routes in the world.

Nepal has a diverse culture with over 100 ethnic groups and more than 90 languages spoken across the country. Major festivals include Dashain, Tihar, Holi, and Eid. The country follows a federal democratic republic system of government. Nepal has made significant progress in areas of education, healthcare, and infrastructure over the past two decades.

The wildlife of Nepal is equally diverse. Chitwan National Park and Bardia National Park are home to Bengal tigers, one-horned rhinoceroses, elephants, and gharial crocodiles. The Himalayan region hosts snow leopards, red pandas, and various species of pheasants. Conservation efforts have helped increase the population of endangered species in recent years.
